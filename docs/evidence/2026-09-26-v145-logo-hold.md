# UPCBadger v1.45 — Logo boundary hold

## Objective
Prevent the oversized ConsoleBadger wordmark in the early Seedance master from
escaping the physical circular display boundary.

## Exact edit
The 12 FPS master was inspected directly.

Problematic sequence:
- M11 / 0.92s: last visually safe wordmark-free frame
- M12..M21 / 1.00..1.75s: oversized wordmark excursion
- M22 / 1.83s: first safe wordmark state

V1.45 keeps exactly 181 output frames:
- 0..11 unchanged
- 12..21 are exact repeated copies of frame 11
- 22..180 unchanged

This creates a deliberate 10-frame hold:
10 / 12 = 0.8333 seconds.

## Integrity
No spatial transform.
No scaling.
No recolour.
No palette change.

All 181 output payloads are byte-for-byte selected from the canonical V1.42
master payloads.
